# Postcode district boundaries

These GeoJSON files are UK postcode **district** boundary polygons (one file per
postcode area, one `Feature` per district within it).

- Source: [missinglink/uk-postcode-polygons](https://github.com/missinglink/uk-postcode-polygons)
- Derived from the KML files linked on Wikipedia's
  [List of postcode districts in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_postcode_districts_in_the_United_Kingdom).
- Wikipedia content is licensed **CC BY-SA**; attribution is retained here and in
  the Coverage map legend.

Boundaries are approximate (crowd-sourced) and intended for visual overview, not
for legal/operational geofencing.
